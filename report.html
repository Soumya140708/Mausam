👥 How Community Reports Can Help

Local weather conditions can sometimes vary across nearby areas.

Community observations can provide additional ground-level context such as:

  • Local waterlogging
  • Heavy rainfall
  • Reduced visibility
  • Strong winds
  • Lightning observations
  • Other unusual weather conditions

In the future, the backend can process these reports and display verified or relevant observations separately from official weather alerts.

Current reports are stored locally for this prototype. They can later be sent to the Python backend and database.
